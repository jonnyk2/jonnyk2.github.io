The studio

Small team, big ambitions

ChaosVibe is one developer directing a crew of AI agents. Design, code, art and testing run side by side every day, so the game moves fast and players see real changes from week to week.

We build in the open: players test early builds in the browser, and their bug reports shape what comes next. Emberwrought is our first game, and we mean for it to grow into a world people share.
